What it does

PressPal turns Camera Control into the Action Button iPhone Duo doesn't have. Press once to open a full-screen camera, and press again to run your chosen action: flashlight, photo, video, Silent Mode, Focus, a voice memo, a timer, or any shortcut. It works from the Lock Screen, so you can turn on the flashlight from your pocket. Lightly press and slide Camera Control to adjust flashlight brightness.

  • Second-press actionPress Camera Control again to run the action you picked.
