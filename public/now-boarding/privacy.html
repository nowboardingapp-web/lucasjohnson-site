Privacy policy · Effective November 6, 2026

Now Boarding privacy

Short version: your flights stay on your device and in your own iCloud. Some flight details are sent to look up live status.

Now Boarding has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Flights you log

The flights, notes and photos you add are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, they may also sync through your private iCloud account, which I can't access.

Calendar, camera and photos

  • Calendar: with your permission, Now Boarding reads your calendars on your device to find flights. Your calendar isn't uploaded.
  • Camera: used only to scan the barcode on a boarding pass. Scanning happens on your device.
  • Photos: used to show the photos you took on each flight. Photos aren't uploaded.
  • Imported confirmations: booking details you paste or share into the app are read on your device.
  • Trip videos: trip videos and their titles are made on your device. A video only leaves your phone if you save or share it.

Live flight updates

Live updates are part of Now Boarding Pro. To show live delays, gates and baggage claim, the app sends Now Boarding's flight-status service the flight number, date and departure airport, plus your seat and gate if you added them. Following a flight on the Lock Screen also sends a token from Apple so the service can update that card. The service asks the flight-data provider AeroDataBox for the flight's status and sends updates through Apple's push service. Your name, contact details and confirmation codes are never sent. A followed flight is deleted automatically within 48 hours.

When a followed flight lands, the service looks up the current weather at the arrival airport using Apple WeatherKit, to show a welcome and the local weather. Only the airport's location is sent, nothing about you.

Without Pro, a Lock Screen card counts down from the scheduled times, and the service makes no flight lookups for it. It only sends the card an “end” message 30 minutes after landing.

Sharing with friends

If you invite friends, Now Boarding uses Apple's iCloud sharing to share your flights with the people you invite. Only the route, date, times and flight number are shared, never confirmation codes, seats, gates, prices, notes or family members' flights. You can stop sharing at any time.

Purchases

Now Boarding Pro is a yearly subscription handled entirely by Apple. I don't receive your payment details. You can manage or cancel it anytime in your Apple Account settings.

Children

Now Boarding isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Delete a flight in the app to remove it from your device and your iCloud. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
