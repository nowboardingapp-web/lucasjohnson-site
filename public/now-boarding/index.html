Support

How do I add flights from my calendar?

Allow calendar access when Now Boarding asks. It looks for flights in your calendars on your phone and lets you choose which ones to add. Your calendar is never uploaded.

How do live flight updates work?

For flights with a flight number, Now Boarding checks the latest status and can show it on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island on the day you fly.

How do I remove ads?

Go to Settings › Remove Ads. It's a one-time purchase. If you reinstall or get a new phone, tap Restore Purchases on the same screen.

How does sharing with friends work?

Invite people from the Friends screen and they'll see your upcoming flights. Only the route, date, times and flight number are shared, never confirmation codes, seats, gates, prices or notes.
